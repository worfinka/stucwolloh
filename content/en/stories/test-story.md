It's like I fell into a world of fairytale fantasies. A world so absurdly blank, hahaha. I'm lost in the haze yet there's an imaginary echo! In a fog so softly humid... That's why I don't feel any pain - only emptiness. Right there hopelessness is finding its way out. But it's not my master... Oh, how it tries to caress me developing light goosebumps of weakness. They unconsciously attack me with a weighty feeling of loneliness. I'm absolutely emotionless, yet somehow on a verge of tears - my favorite activity. I seem tired of everything but don't feel like yawning yet. My life's got better but somewhere deep in my soul things that I've been put through with years of suffering are coming out. It's annihilating me... No... Maybe, it's the opposite - the start of a war. I was eaten by the dark and lived in it for quite a time. It became my closest friend. I felt myself normal. Actually normal. Because I thought it's how it should be. Was I truly happy? Maybe somewhere inside me the troops of hope are holding the roll call. Maybe, I should help them. But it's easier for me to just dismantle them. DO NOT GIVE THEM ANY CHANCE TO EXIST. I'm close to bliss, yet so far away at the same time... It's so hard for me to even try achieving it. One who felt true happiness can feel dead inner misery. In turn, the spawn clad in dark armor will refuse to take it off. I'm like a man who became a vampire and already got used to a new, nightly life. I'm too weak to step outside, to feel the rays of that sweet little sun. And this feeling is killing me. It's both my comrade and enemy. Helplessness, loneliness, self‑loathing... I ruin my own life. But I do it beautifully, tearing myself apart with an endless spectrum of emotions! I'll call it a spectacle of devastation. A grand performance worthy only of a wooden box. And now, good night. I feel like sleeping.

Every evening is like the same script. I find a moment for quiet relaxation, and again I'm overwhelmed by a sense of hopelessness mixed with lost beauty. A past not so distant, yet so different. Can't say it was carefree – it was just different... Now, having been through all that... What's yet to come scares me. Walking along the old paths you've trodden, you'll know for sure you're going the right way. There are moments I'd like to be in again. Just live them once more. Roughly, the last year. To trample that path for my own great self. This year had bad moments, but what happened – I still want to repeat it. And I know that in the future something more beautiful, pleasant, warm may await me – but it's not the same. A heavy blanket. You pull it over your head. But it suffocates you. I'm a nobody... If only I could illegally drown in the past.

Every time... I hate this feeling. But at the same time it makes me write. A sick feeling of incompleteness, a desire for more. Whether it's emotional friendship or crazy love. As if it's caressing you, but actually twisting you from the inside. Your last juices are drained while waiting for something inevitable. And you realize how powerless you are. You can't even take a step further. Circumstances only make everything harder. And you're like a wanderer traveling through the same trashy streets, trying to figure out who you are. It seems I need attention, but I'm afraid... Afraid of something.

Don't flatter yourself. You need to fight. I'm stuck in a cycle of mental chaos. The stone I've been kicking all along has finally hit a dead end – so it's time to move it right or left. A little more, it seems I'm getting closer. But night always tightens; a good sleep is the limit of my dreams.
Wait. But you still haven't made a choice. Right or left after all? Wake up. You're stuck again in your haze, your toy emptiness. There's always a way out. Do you still hear the echo? It's your inner voice, no one else's. Say goodbye to it. I believe I can... Good night, I feel like sleeping.

Morning gives new strength, but it's just an illusion. It feels awkward. It's like I'm saying goodbye to my body for a while. Waiting in line at the checkout or trying to enjoy a walk. In those moments, sometimes unexpectedly, I become an actor in my own theater. And I'm not playing the lead role. I manage to build a stage in my imagination: I'm standing somewhere at its edge, and all the spotlights are pointed the other way. The audience won't clap or laugh. They aren't there. There's only me and what the spotlights are blindingly illuminating. What holds my attention awakens memories lying on the surface. No matter how I try to sink them, they still surface. Maybe because they're empty inside...?

I feel the distance I created. I'm guilty of ruining a beautiful friendship, turning it into a chase after myself. It's a game for which I wrote the rules on my own. The main prize here is pain. And actually, I understand everything. I understand that the one to blame is only me. At least I want to believe that. And this very belief shows how pathetic I am. I'd like to end on a good note, but it's hard for now. I'll lie down, I feel like sleeping.

I look for the one I need in other people. More precisely, the one whose existence I need. I see a person and I want it to be her. Or I don't want to – probably I'm afraid. After all, I wouldn't approach. I'd just wave and put on a friendly face. Maybe I just need to run away? Somewhere far away, where nothing reminds me of her? In that case, I'll soon go crazy. I'm already fed up with all these metaphors and beautiful images – every day the world I created becomes more and more empty. Time will pass, and nothing will remain here, not even my thoughts. No desire to sleep... I wouldn't approach. I'd just wave and put on a friendly face. But she would. And I'm stuck imagining what would happen next, what could have been before. That's what kills me. I don't know, and this very ignorance I can squeeze endlessly. But it's worth nothing.

Good morning. Or day? Maybe evening? Doesn't matter. And what do I do now? I've already understood everything – and this text has exhausted its need to exist. I wrote it for reflection, self‑deception, analysis of my delicate situation. But the stone can't just stay between two roads. Some passerby will surely kick it onto one of them. All I have to do is wait for – or find – that kind person. And it won't be her.
I could go on describing this state for a long time, until I run out of all genuine words, but I prefer to be free. You can insert a symbolic farewell to all the images that bind me here – I'm not up to it anymore.
